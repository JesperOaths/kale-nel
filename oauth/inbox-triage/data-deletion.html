Data deletion & revocation

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Revoke Google access

You can stop future Gmail API access at any time by opening your Google Account settings, going to your third-party connections/security permissions, selecting Inbox Triage Agent (or the name displayed on the Google consent screen), and removing its access. Once revoked, the service can no longer obtain new Gmail API access tokens for that account without a new authorization.

Request deletion from the private service

To request deletion of the locally retained authorization credentials and operational state associated with an authorized Gmail account, email prutsalberts@gmail.com with the subject Inbox Triage Agent data deletion and identify the Google account whose local service data should be removed.

The deletion request covers locally stored OAuth credentials and service state associated with the account. Operational backups or diagnostic records, if present, may persist temporarily until their normal rotation/retention cycle completes, but they are not used to continue Gmail access after revocation.

What revocation does not do

Revoking OAuth does not delete email already stored in Gmail and does not retract a digest or message that was previously delivered to the account owner's configured private destination. Those systems have their own deletion controls.

Questions

For help with revocation or deletion, contact prutsalberts@gmail.com.
