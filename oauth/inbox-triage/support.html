Support

Inbox Triage Agent is a private Kalenel productivity utility for specifically authorized account owners.

Contact

For authorization problems, privacy questions, data deletion requests or service issues, contact prutsalberts@gmail.com.

Google authorization problems

If Google access was revoked or expired, the account owner must authorize the application again through Google's OAuth consent flow. The application never asks for or stores the user's Google password.

Privacy and data controls

See the Privacy Policy for how Google user data is handled and the Data deletion & revocation page for instructions to remove access and locally retained service data.
