Play Tune Rush on your laptop while your Samsung S21 runs the music service, track selection, scoring and game progress. No multiplayer room or second player required.

CONNECT TO YOUR S21

Start your solo session

Your phone and laptop must be able to reach each other on the local network, and the Tune Rush Solo service must be running in Termux on the S21.

Uses port 8797. The hostname is a convenient default; if it doesn't resolve from your laptop, enter your S21's current Wi-Fi IP or IPv6 address instead. The address is saved only in this browser.
