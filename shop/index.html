Style direction

Natural detail, not noise.

Bruis is for quiet nature graphics: refined botanical forms, marine life, insects, flowers, leaves, and small creatures on clean shirts. The pieces should feel easy to wear first, then reward a closer look.

The collection avoids heavy slogans and loud branding. Think pressed flowers, pond creatures, greenhouse details, soft wings, stems, petals, shells, and field-study illustrations.

✿Flowers

Hydrangea, thistle, lace-like stems, petals and soft garden forms.

🪲Small creatures

Mantis, fly, axolotl and other odd little subjects with character.

〰Water life

Jellyfish, pond moods, drifting shapes and calm marine colour.
