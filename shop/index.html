Style direction

Natural detail, not noise.

Bruis is for quiet nature graphics: refined botanical forms, marine life, insects, and small creatures on clean shirts. The designs are meant to feel wearable first, then interesting up close.

The collection avoids heavy slogans and oversized loud branding. It is simple, soft, and a bit unusual.
