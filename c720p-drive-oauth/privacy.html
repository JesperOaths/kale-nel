Privacy Policy

C720P Drive Backup · Effective 16 September 2026

Purpose and operator

C720P Drive Backup is a private utility operated by the owner of the C720P home hub. It is used only to back up and retrieve the owner's own hub recovery files and camera-archive data in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google user data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. This permission is used only to create, read, update, organize, download, and delete Drive files and folders that the application creates or that the owner explicitly makes available to the application.

How data is used

Google user data is used solely to provide the backup, restore, integrity-verification, and archive-management functions requested by the owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, credit decisions, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's C720P device with restricted file permissions. Backup files remain in the owner's own Google Drive account. The application does not intentionally transmit Google user data to third-party advertising or data-broker services.

Sharing and sale

Google user data is not sold, rented, licensed, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data may only be processed by Google Drive as necessary to provide the requested storage functionality.

Retention and deletion

Files remain in Google Drive until the owner deletes them or the application's retention rules remove them. The owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time through their Google Account permissions. Local OAuth credentials can also be deleted from the C720P device to end access.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the application's use of Google data can be sent to prutsalberts@gmail.com.
