C720P Drive Backup

Private Google Drive backup utility for the owner's C720P home hub.

Purpose. C720P Drive Backup securely stores recovery archives and camera-archive data from the owner's C720P home hub in the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google Drive access. The application requests the narrow drive.file permission. It uses that permission only for Drive files and folders that the application creates or that the owner explicitly makes available to it.

No public service. This is a personal utility. It is not offered as a consumer service and does not sell, rent, advertise with, or disclose Google user data to third parties.
